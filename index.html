Main Screen Toolbar & Actions

+ Auto Account Add

When Riot Client is logged in, clicking the plus button automatically detects the active summoner name, level, server, and rank badge without typing.

144Hz Live Hz & Power Switcher

Instantly toggles your monitor's display refresh rate (e.g. 144Hz/240Hz for gaming, 60Hz for desktop) and Windows Power Plan via direct Win32 APIs.

Locks game.cfg and PersistedSettings.json as read-only. Prevents League updates from wiping custom keybinds.

Cleanly terminates League of Legends, Riot Client, Vanguard tray services, and companion overlay apps in one swift operation.

🎮 Quick LoL Launcher

Move profile cards up or down using the arrow buttons to arrange your most-played accounts right at the top.

📋 Click-to-Copy Riot ID

🔍 Porofessor Match History
